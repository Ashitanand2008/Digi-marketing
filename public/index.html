Our story

Rooted in Nature.
Made for Everyday Life.

EarthNest began with a question about an ordinary Bengaluru errand. What if the bag you took to the market, the bottle in your backpack and the cloth beside your sink were things you could reach for again tomorrow?

We’re a conceptual Bengaluru small business making reusable household choices easier to find and easier to afford. We choose practical alternatives to disposables and share ways to care for them. Our idea of a community starts locally, with neighbours exchanging small habits that work.

Our philosophy

Sustainability
Starts at Home.

A few familiar habits can make everyday purchasing more thoughtful. Start with one that fits the way you live.

Use what you already own

Keep a tote by the door and your bottle beside your keys. Give the things you already own a regular place in your day.

Choose refills with care

When a neighbourhood shop offers refills, take a clean jar or bottle. Reusing it for soap or pantry staples can save a disposable pack.

Help everyday items last

Air-dry cloths and bamboo after washing, and mend loose tote handles. When you need a replacement, look for sturdy seams, washable materials or a lid you can replace separately.
